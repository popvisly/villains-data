# Captori Knowledge Base: Architectural & Strategic Decisions

## 1. Decision-First Architecture (Feb 2026)

### Strategic Pivot: From "AI Career" to "Judgment Moat"

- **Context**: Market saturation of generic AI career tools created a "commoditization trap."
- **Decision**: Pivot branding and infrastructure to focus on "Human Judgment" (discretionary logic) as the primary value driver.
- **Rationale**: Human judgment is the most resilient asset in the 2026 labor market (ref: Global Labour Resilience Index 2025).

### Antigravity Alignment: Mission Control

- **Context**: The Antigravity platform provides autonomous agent orchestration capabilities.
- **Decision**: Treat the environment as a "Mission Control" rather than an editor. Use "Skills" to hard-code proprietary resilience logic.
- **Implementation**: Created `skills/resilience-index` to triage tasks based on "Contextual Understanding" and "Discretionary Accountability."

## 2. UI/UX: Soft UI Evolution

### Neumorphic Design System

- **Context**: Need to differentiate from "flat" AI software and create a sense of tactile gravity/trust.
- **Decision**: Implement Neumorphism (Soft UI) using light gradients and soft shadows.
- **Visual Goal**: Components should feel like physical, high-stakes dashboard elements ("Mission Control").

## 3. Resilience Index Grounding

### Global Labour Resilience Index 2025

- **Context**: Users need defensible metrics for career planning.
- **Decision**: Ground the "Resilience Index" in 2025 metrics, specifically weighting "Emotional Intelligence," "Negotiation," and "Strategic Constraint Mapping."

## 5. Fiscal Optimization: R&D Tax Incentive (R&DTI)

### Refundable Tax Offset

- **Context**: Captori is a software startup with <$20m turnover.
- **Decision**: Aim for the 43.5% refundable tax offset by documenting novel algorithm development.
- **R&D Activities**:
  - Development of the **Resilience Index** algorithm (Absorptive/Transformative capacity).
  - Implementing the **W3C Verifiable Credentials** bridge for "Proof of Judgment."
- **Contemporaneous Documentation**: Maintain a rigorous log of technological uncertainties and experimentation in `scripts/rd-audit-log.json`.

## 6. Institutional Framework: LaunchVic (Victoria, Australia)

### Victoria Startup Strategy 2024–2028

- **Context**: LaunchVic supports AI and DeepTech high-growth sectors.
- **Decision**: Integrate with the Victorian ecosystem by aligning with LaunchVic-supported accelerators and investor networks.
- **Strategy**: Leverage state-backed infrastructure to reach early customers and secure faster capital infusion.

## 7. UI/UX Evolution: Soft UI 2.0

### Accessibility & Contrast

- **Context**: Early Neumorphic iterations faced visibility challenges.
- **Decision**: Transition to **Soft UI 2.0** (Tactile Minimalist Realistic).
- **Implementation**: Enhancement of shadows (`neumorphic-2.0`) and high-contrast text layers to ensure "Zero-Ambiguity" for professional users.

## 8. Data Governance: Trusted Career Profile (TCP)

### W3C & Selective Disclosure

- **Context**: Professionals need to share verified skills without exposing full career history.
- **Decision**: Align with TCP standards using **Selective Disclosure** in the Verifiable Credentials bridge.
- **Privacy**: Adhere to the 13 **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)** via decentralized identifiers (DIDs).
