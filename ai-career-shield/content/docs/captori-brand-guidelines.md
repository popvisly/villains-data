# Captori Brand Guidelines

This document serves as the definitive visual and tonal reference for Captori. NotebookLM should use these guidelines to maintain brand consistency when generating marketing assets, infographics, or strategic content.

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

- **Primary Blue:** `#6366F1` (Indigo-500) - Primary brand color for highlights and accents.
- **Secondary White/Off-White:** `#F5F3FF` - Used for clean backgrounds and secondary text.
- **Accent Teal:** `#10B981` (Emerald-500) - Reserved for success states, "Safe" zones, and CTA highlights.
- **Background:** Soft gradients using `#020617` (Slate-950) or deep indigos. Never use solid black.
- **Avoid:** Red (unless indicating urgent risk) and primary green (use the brand Teal/Emerald instead).

### Typography

- **Headlines:** **Lora** (Serif, Bold, Tracking-tight). Used to convey authority, judgment, and premium quality.
- **Body Text:** **Inter** (Sans-serif, Medium weight, 400-500). Optimized for readability and professional clarity.
- **UI Elements:** **Inter** (Semi-bold, 600) for buttons, labels, and micro-copy.
- **Constraints:** Never use default system fonts or condensed/playful fonts.

### Design Style

- **Aesthetic:** Soft UI with Glass-morphism. Use translucent panels (`bg-white/5` or `bg-slate-900/50`) with backdrop-blur.
- **Shadows:** Subtle, diffused shadows (not harsh drop shadows).
- **Corners:** Rounded corners (8px for components, 16px-32px for large cards/panels).
- **Spacing:** Strict **8pt grid system** (8px, 16px, 24px, 32px increments).
- **Overall Feel:** Premium, modern, professional. It should feel like a "High-End Career Advisor," not a corporate tool.

### Visual Metaphors

- **Recommended:** Balance scales (judgment), compasses (direction), building blocks (proof), upward arrows (acceleration), shields (resilience).
- **Avoid:** Binary "Humans vs. Robots" imagery, alarmist warning triangles, or generic "AI brain" icons.

### Tone & Voice

- **Empowering:** Focus on what professionals *gain* (leverage, moat) rather than what they lose.
- **Data-Driven:** Ground claims in logic, metrics, and the "Resilience Index."
- **Direct & Grounded:** Avoid tech hype or over-promising. Use professional, sober language.

## Infographic-Specific Guidelines

### Layout & Composition

- **Format:** Vertical (1080x1350px) for LinkedIn/Instagram optimization.
- **Top 20%:** **The Hook.** Large Lora headline. Validates the user's primary concern.
- **Middle 60%:** **Core Narrative.** 2-3 distinct sections (e.g., Comparison, Matrix, or Gauge).
- **Bottom 20%:** **The Call to Action.** High-contrast branding and the URL.

### Content Hierarchy

- **Level 1:** Large, bold Lora (72pt+) for primary hooks.
- **Level 2:** Inter Semi-bold (36pt-48pt) for section headers.
- **Level 3:** Inter Regular/Medium (24pt-30pt) for supporting copy.

### Call to Action (CTA)

- **Primary Labels:** "Calculate Your Resilience Index" or "Generate Your 90-Day Plan."
- **Button Style:** Rounded, Primary Blue (`#6366F1`) or Indigo-600 background with white text.
- **Link Requirement:** Always include `captori.ai/start` or `captori.com` in the footer.

## Examples of On-Brand vs Off-Brand

| Feature | On-Brand ✅ | Off-Brand ❌ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Colors** | Indigo, Teal, Soft Gradients | Traffic-Light Green/Red, Solid Black |
| **Typography** | Lora + Inter | Arial, Comic Sans, Impact |
| **Tone** | "Build Your Career Moat" | "AI is Taking Your Job" |
| **Visuals** | Glass panels, soft shadows | Flat 2D boxes, harsh borders |
