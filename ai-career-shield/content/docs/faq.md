# Captori FAQ

## Pre-Purchase (Why Buy)

- **How is this different from generic career coaching?** Career coaching is $200+/hour for human advice. Captori gives you a personalized 30/60/90 plan for $39, based on your specific role architecture.
- **Can I get a refund if it doesn't help?** Yes. If you execute the plan and don't see value, email us within 30 days.
- **Do I need to quit my job to use this?** No. The plan is designed to execute alongside a full-time job (2-5 hours/week).
- **How long does it take to see results?** 30 days for first artifact, 60 days to templatize, 90 days for evidence.
- **Is this just for people worried about losing their jobs?** No. It's for mid-career professionals wanting to build leverage and own positioning.

## Post-Purchase (Getting Started)

- **I got my plan, now what?** Start with "This Week" (page 2). Block 2 hours to document how you make trade-offs.
- **How do I use this with my existing tools?** Export milestones to Notion or Jira. The plan tells you WHAT to build; your tools track WHEN.
- **Can I customize my plan?** Yes. Adapt milestones to your specific context (e.g., "Build a framework" &rarr; "Build How I Score Features doc").

## Technical Issues

- **I didn't receive my plan email.** Check spam, then email <support@captori.com>.
- **Can I download this as a PDF?** Yes, it is delivered as a PDF attachment.
- **My Resilience Index seems wrong. Can I retake?** Yes, go to /start and retake. Results depend on task mix, discretion, and complexity inputs.

## Payment & Billing

- **Is this a subscription?** No. One-time payment ($39 or $99).
- **Can I upgrade from $39 to $99?** Yes, email <support@captori.com> for a difference payment link.
- **Can I buy this for my team?** Yes, email for team pricing (5+ members).

## Product & Roadmap

- **When will Module 4 (Capacity) be released?** Scheduled for Q2 2025.
- **Can I get updates to my plan as AI changes?** Quarterly market updates available. Refresh for $29.

## Philosophy & Approach

- **What is "Grounded AI"?** Approach: (1) Draft before prompting, (2) No infinite loops, (3) Protected thinking time (90 min).
- **Why no productivity features?** Intentional. We focus on WHAT to build, not tracking it.
