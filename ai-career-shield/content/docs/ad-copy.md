# Captori Ad Asset Pack

*Targets: career development plan, PM career growth, AI job risk.*

- **Headline 1**: Is Your Role AI-Resilient?
- **Headline 2**: Get Your Career Roadmap
- **Headline 3**: Captori Career Strategy
- **Description**: Stop guessing your job security. Get a data-driven 90-day resilience plan in 2 minutes. No credit card required.

### Variation 2: Outcome-Oriented

- **Headline 1**: 30/60/90 Day Career Plan

- **Headline 3**: Captori Career Engine

## 2. LinkedIn Ads (Professional/B2B)

*Targets: Senior PMs, Engineering Leads, Strategy Consultants.*

### Asset 1: The "Seniority Compression"

- **Primary Text**: Seniority isn't about context anymore. AI provides that. Seniority is now about consequence. Does your career plan reflect the shift?
- **Headline**: Generate Your AI Resilience Index
- **Visual Direction**: Chart showing "Volume" (decreasing value) vs. "Judgment" (increasing value).

### Asset 2: The "Decision Log"

- **Primary Text**: Stop shipping Jira tickets. Start shipping proof of judgment. Captori gives you the templates and the roadmap to do it.
- **Headline**: The Resume is Dead. Long Live the Proof Portfolio.
- **Visual Direction**: Screen recording of the Captori Example Plan (Decision Log focus).

## 3. Reddit Ads (Community/Native)

*Target Subreddits: r/ProductManagement, r/Consulting, r/CareerAdvice.*

### Asset 1: Community Feedback

- **Headline**: I built a tool that analyzes PM tasks vs AI capabilities. It gives you a tactical 90-day plan to stay relevant.
- **Body**: No fluff, no "coaching" calls. Just a 2-minute assessment that tells you where your role is vulnerable and how to build a judgment moat. Free starter plan available.
- **Link**: captori.ai/start

## Creative Direction

- **Vibe**: Calm, premium, "Soft UI" aesthetic.
- **Imagery**: Dashboard screenshots or clean workspace macro-shots. No smiling stock photos.
- **Colors**: Indigo-900 with Emerald accents.
