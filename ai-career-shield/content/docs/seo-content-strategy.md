# Master SEO & Content Strategy

## 1. Core Philosophy: The Cluster Model

Captori uses a **Topic Cluster** model centered around "Career Resilience for the AI Era."

- **Pillar Content**: Home, Example, Start Assessment.
- **Cluster Content**: 15 targeted blog posts addressing specific professional anxieties and tactical needs.

## 2. Content Garden (The 15 Posts)

The content library is designed to capture high-intent traffic across three categories:

- **Awareness (Risk)**: AI job risk, role commoditization, replacement anxiety.
- **Consideration (Templates)**: Career plans, leverage maps, operation plans.
- **Decision (Proof)**: Portfolios, interviews, judgment artifacts.

## 3. SEO Maturity Roadmap

| Phase | Duration | Focus | Key KPI |
|-------|----------|-------|---------|
| 1: Indexing | Week 1-4 | Technical hygiene & GSC submission. | Search Console activity. |
| 2: Authority | Month 2-3 | Internal linking & early backlinks. | Top 50 rankings. |
| 3: Conversion | Month 4-6 | Optimizing CTAs & email capture. | Assessment completions. |
| 4: Breakthrough | Month 6-12 | Ranking for "Career Plan" & "AI Job Risk". | $1,000+ MRR. |

## 4. Grounded SEO Principles

- **No Slop**: Every post must include a tactical artifact or framework (e.g., "The Decision Log").
- **Asset focus**: Content itself is an asset. Avoid shallow "10 tips" styles.
- **Strategic Linking**: All roads lead to `/example` (The Proof Page) to demonstrate the product's value high-fidelity.

## 5. Performance Benchmarks

- **Target Top 3**: "AI Job Risk Assessment", "Career Development Plan Template".
- **Conversion Goal**: 5% of blog visitors enter the `/start` funnel.
- **Retention Goal**: 15% of Free Assessment takers upgrade to Growth/Executive within 90 days via email nurture.

## 6. Maintenance Protocol

- **Quarterly Refresh**: Update stats on "Automation Spectrum" every 90 days.
- **Community seeding**: One Reddit/LinkedIn share per new post.
- **Backlink outreach**: Focus on career boutique agencies and PM/Strategy communities.
