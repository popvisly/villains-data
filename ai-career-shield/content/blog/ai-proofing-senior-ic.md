# AI-Proofing Your Role as a Senior IC

The "Senior Individual Contributor" track is being squeezed by AI. When a junior dev with an AI agent can produce as much volume as a senior, the seniority gap narrows—unless you shift your value point.

## The Seniority Compression

Historically, seniority was tied to **Volume of Context**. Today, AI provides context to juniors instantly. To remain "Senior," you must now be tied to **Consequence of Choice**.

## The Senior IC Moat: Judgment Artifacts

Stop optimizing for code/tickets. Start optimizing for:

- **Architecture Constraints**: Defining the 'Why' behind technical bets.
- **Risk Mitigation**: Identifying shifts AI's historical data won't catch.
- **Political Buy-In**: Navigating human incentives to ship complex features.

## The Executive Strategy

Moving toward Staff or Principal levels in 2025 requires a **Proof Portfolio**. You need evidence that you made the decision that saved the project millions in debt.
