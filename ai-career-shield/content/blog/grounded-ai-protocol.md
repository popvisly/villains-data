# The Grounded AI Protocol for Professionals

The greatest risk of AI isn't that it replaces you. It's that it **hollows you out**. If you let AI do the thinking, you lose the "Judgment Muscle" that makes you valuable.

## What is Grounded AI?

Grounded AI is a set of protocols ensuring human judgment remains the anchor of the work, while AI acts as the engine.

### Protocol 1: Draft Before Prompt

Never start with an empty prompt. Always draft the **Logic Architecture** yourself first. AI should fill the volume, not define the direction.

### Protocol 2: The Discretion Gap

Identify the "Grey Areas" where data is missing or stakeholders are conflicted. Tell AI to ignore these so you can apply human judgment.

### Protocol 3: Verification of Consequence

Every AI output must be audited for political and strategic alignment. You must sign off as the "Consequence Owner."

## Operator vs. Owner

Operators use AI to get more work done. Owners use AI to spend more time thinking about high-impact decisions.
