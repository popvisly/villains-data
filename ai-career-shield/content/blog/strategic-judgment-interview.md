# How to Show Strategic Judgment in Interviews

Interviews are shifting. Hiring managers are becoming wary of the "polished storyteller" because stories can be rehearsed (or prompted). They want to see **Proof of Work**.

## The Death of the STAR Method

The STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) method is built on claims. In the AI era, recruiters are looking for the "Deep Work" behind the claims. They want to know *how* you decided, not just what you did.

## The "Artifact" Response Strategy

When asked a behavioral question, don't just speak. If the interview is remote, offer to share a **Decision Architecture** document. If in person, describe your **Constraint Map**.

### Claim vs. Proof

- **Claim (Weak)**: "I led the redesign and it resulted in a 40% uptick in conversion."
- **Proof (Strong)**: "I can walk you through the Decision Log from the redesign. We actually ignored the top-requested user feature because the political risk of the pivot was too high..."

## 3 Artifacts to Bring to Every Interview

1. **The Constraint Map**: Analysis of how you navigated technical or political boundaries.
2. **The Decision Architecture**: A breakdown of a high-risk bet and the signals used.
3. **The Judgment Audit**: Your 90-day Resilience Index (proves awareness of leverage).
