# From 'Doing' to 'Deciding': The AI Career Shift

The industrial era was about standardization. The digital era was about information. The AI era is about **Discretion**.

## The Erosion of "Doing"

Today, an AI agent can do 80% of "doing" activities at zero marginal cost. Many professionals are still trying to compete on volume—a losing strategy.

> "AI removes the cost of execution, making the value of judgment infinite."

## The Automation Pivot

Move away from "Volume Tasks" and toward "Decision Tasks":

- **The 'Doing' Trap**: Repetitive aggregation, formatting, standardized drafts.
- **The 'Deciding' Moat**: Stakeholder alignment, risk tolerance modeling, strategic synthesis.
