# The Future-Proof Your Career Checklist (2025 Edition)

The market is shifting faster than the education cycle. To stay ahead, you need to audit your role twice a year against the **Reality of Automation**.

## The 10-Point Career Audit

1. **Decision Ownership**: Do I own a decision that AI cannot make?
2. **Proof Artifacts**: Have I shipped a 'Proof Artifact' this month?
3. **Owner vs. Operator**: Does my leadership see me as an 'Owner'?
4. **Prompt Simulation**: Can my primary deliverable be simulated with a prompt?
5. **Human Map**: Do I have a 'Human Map' of my stakeholders?
6. **Grounded AI**: Am I using 'Grounded AI' protocols?
7. **Presence vs. Proof**: Is my network built on 'Proof'?
8. **Operation Plan**: Do I have a 30/60/90 Day Operation Plan?
9. **Ambiguity Move**: Am I moving toward ambiguity?
10. **Remote Test**: Would my role exist if the company went remote?

## Results Tracking

If you answered 'No' to more than 3, your career resilience score is likely in the danger zone.
