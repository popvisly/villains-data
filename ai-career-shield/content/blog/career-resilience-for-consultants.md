# Career Resilience for Senior Consultants

The consulting model is under siege. Clients are no longer willing to pay for "slides and research" that they can generate with a prompt. The value is shifting to **Implementation Judgment**.

## The Death of the Research Phase

If you charge for information aggregation, your revenue is at risk. Your new value point is the **Synthesis of Constraints**—telling a client why the AI's consensus is actually wrong for their specific reality.

## Consultant Resilience Benchmarks

- **80% Discretion**: Focus on risk tolerance and stakeholder ego management.
- **Asset-Based Billing**: Charge for "Judgment Artifacts" rather than hours.
- **AI Orchestration**: Manage the AI agents that do the low-level volume work.
- **Political Moat**: Double down on human relationships AI cannot navigate.
