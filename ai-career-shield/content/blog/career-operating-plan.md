# Why You Need a Career Operating Plan

Most professionals treat their career like a side effect of their job. They work hard, wait for a review, and hope for a promotion. This is a **Passive Growth** model, and it's dangerous.

## What is a Career Operating Plan?

A Career Operating Plan (COP) is a technical document defining the inputs and assets you need to ensure your role remains judgment-heavy.

### The COP Architecture

- **Input Audit**: Filtering daily tasks to maximize high-consequence judgment.
- **Asset Log**: A prioritized list of 'Proof Artifacts' you intend to ship.
- **Resilience Benchmarks**: Target scores for automation exposure and leverage.

## Owning the Workflow

A COP ensures that even if AI automates a chunk of your department, your **Value Architecture** remains intact. You aren't just doing a job; you are running an operation.
