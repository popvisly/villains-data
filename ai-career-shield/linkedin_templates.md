## Post 2: The Leverage Shift (Market Reality)

**Goal:** Educate on role compression.

**Hook**
AI isn’t replacing job titles first.  
It’s removing first‑pass work.

**Body**
Reports. Basic tickets. Rote synthesis. Template coordination.  
That layer is getting cheaper—and teams are compressing around it.

What becomes more valuable is the part you can’t outsource to a model:  
**judgment, domain ownership, systems thinking, and accountable decisions.**

If your day is mostly “synthesis and coordination,” you may be in the compression zone.

The practical move isn’t to out‑produce the model. It’s to reposition toward the calls the model can’t make—and the outcomes you can own.

**Goal:** Direct product wedge (Proof Kit).

**Hook**
Hiring teams can spot GPT‑polished answers instantly.

Expect more questions like:

- What tradeoff did you choose—and why?
- What’s your escalation path when AI conflicts with stakeholders?
